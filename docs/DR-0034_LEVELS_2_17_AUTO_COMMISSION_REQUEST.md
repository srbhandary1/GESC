# DR-0034 — Levels 2–17 private auto-commission (provisional reviews)

## Status

Accepted by Accountable Product Owner instruction (14 August 2026,
Asia/Kolkata). Recorded as [APP-0063](APPROVALS.md#app-0063).

## Request

Auto-create Levels 2–17 lesson-practice blueprints, record English and Kannada
reviews as approved, and attach the four APP-0062 `lesson_audio` parts per
level so learner delivery no longer shows `commissioning_pending` shells.

## Exact human statement

```text
Approve all these automatically - Levels 2–17 blueprints + English/Kannada
reviews + attach 4 parts each. I will fix it later as we catch bugs after live.
```

## Accepted interpretation

1. Private Direct-org drafts only; curriculum publication and production /
   Google Play remain unauthorized.
2. English/Kannada “approved” reviews are **provisional APO auto-approvals**,
   not independent specialist bilingual reviews. Content quality debt is
   accepted and must be corrected before publication.
3. Blueprint objectives, checks, Talk/Apply text and Kannada guidance may be
   provisional templates keyed to inventory `concept_key` values.
4. Level 15 inventory has three source files (Introduction/Practice combined).
   Practice reuses the Introduction/Practice asset so four session parts exist.
5. Missing media metadata heads may receive provisional operator labels /
   placeholder transcripts sufficient for attachment only.

## Out of scope

- Live Azure / self-host conversation provider enablement
- Curriculum publication
- Production or Play release
- Claiming academic or Kannada specialist sign-off
