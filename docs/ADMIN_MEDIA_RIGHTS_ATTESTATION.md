## Bounded interpretation

The statement is durable owner-level evidence that the accountable product
owner asserts control of the media they supply or designate for this project.
It authorizes drafting the next ordered ADMIN-MEDIA-01 checkpoint and may be
referenced as an umbrella rights-holder or ownership-basis document.

It does not by itself:

- identify or approve an unknown file;
- prove that a stored object still has the asserted bytes;
- choose a program, release channel, territory, effective period, sharing or
  derivative-use scope for an asset;
- replace malware scanning, media validation or accessibility review;
- create a `rights_confirmer` appointment, organization permission or runtime
  rights revision;
- approve copied reference-site media, legacy production import, academic or
  Kannada content, deployment, production delivery or public release; or
- waive ADR-0004's exact asset-hash, appointment and confirmation predicates.

Each usable asset therefore remains subject to an exact server-computed
SHA-256, private quarantine and clean scan, and a program-scoped append-only
rights revision confirmed by an active appointed human. The attestation may
be the evidence source for that confirmation, but the confirmation must still
record the exact asset and allowed use.
