To remove optional conversation history from this phone, use Account → Privacy → Delete all saved conversations, or clear the app’s storage. Remote deletion cannot reach an offline phone.

Delete your account

To delete your Global Spoken English App learner account, sign in and ask an administrator in your organisation. If they published a phone, email or WhatsApp on the 1:1 page, you may use that contact. We do not publish a support inbox on this page.

Staff complete erasure with the existing operator privacy command (password confirmation, organisation-scoped). That ends the learner membership. It does not silently delete every global login if you also belong to another organisation. Marks and other immutable evidence stay as recorded in the privacy inventory.
