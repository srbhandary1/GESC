Guest-home Listen samples
=========================

These three WAV files are product-owned Azure Neural TTS clips of the Level 12
demo phrases already published in `demoLevelFixtures.json`. They were
synthesised once with `en-IN-NeerjaNeural` (the ordinary guidance-TTS voice)
so unsigned `#home` can play a fixed file instead of calling Azure or
`/demo/tts` on every Listen click.

They are not learner recordings and must not be replaced with unlicensed
third-party media.
