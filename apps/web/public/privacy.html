Privacy

Global Spoken English App uses your microphone so you can practise speaking. Your recording is sent to Sarvam to turn speech into text (Azure Speech is the failover). Azure then writes Swathi’s reply and may speak it aloud. We do not sell recordings. Source audio is kept only for a short deletion window, not as a permanent lesson archive.

This product does not promise fluency, exam scores, or a CEFR level. Staff in your organisation can export or erase your learner membership through the operator privacy tools after you ask them.
