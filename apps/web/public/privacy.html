Privacy

Global Spoken English App uses your microphone so you can practise speaking. Your recording is sent privately to approved speech-processing providers to turn speech into text (including a bounded failover route). An approved automated conversation provider then writes Swathi’s reply and may speak it aloud. We do not sell recordings. Source audio is kept only for a short deletion window, not as a permanent lesson archive.

This product does not promise fluency, exam scores, or a CEFR level. Staff in your organisation can export or erase your learner membership through the operator privacy tools after you ask them.
