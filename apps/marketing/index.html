The 17 levels

The institute course has 17 speaking levels. The twelve themes below are a public overview of that path — from first greetings to professional debates. Placement puts you on the level that challenges you, never the one that overwhelms you.

  1. 1

    Greetings

    Meet people and open a conversation.

  2. 2

    Talk about yourself with ease.

  3. 3

    Handle routines and everyday errands.

  4. 4

    Order, modify, and make small talk.

  5. 5

    Ask for help and find your way.

  6. 6

    Phone calls

    Speak clearly when you cannot see faces.

  7. 7

  8. 8

    Hold a professional discussion under pressure.
